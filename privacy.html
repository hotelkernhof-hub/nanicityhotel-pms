🏨 Nani City Hotel – Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Data We Collect

When you use our WhatsApp concierge service, we collect:

  • Your WhatsApp phone number
  • Messages you send to our bot
  • Check-in information (name, date of birth, document type, nationality, email, phone)
  • Booking details (arrival and departure dates, room, number of guests)

2. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Provide automated concierge assistance via WhatsApp
  • Process your hotel check-in
  • Send you your room access code
  • Process payments for your stay and optional services
  • Improve our service

3. Data Storage

Your data is stored securely in Google Firebase Firestore. Conversation history is kept for 30 days and then deleted.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share data only with the service providers needed to operate our booking and access systems – our payment provider (SumUp) for payment processing and our smart lock provider for generating your door code – and where required by Swiss law.

5. Your Rights

Under Swiss data protection law (DSG), you have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data. Contact us at: nanicityhotel@gmail.com
